# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This README provides a single entry point to the OctoAcme project management process documents and a short summary of the processes used by the team.

## Overview

OctoAcme follows a structured, iterative approach to project management that emphasizes customer value, clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and psychological safety. Our methodology spans the complete project lifecycle—from initial idea validation through execution, release, and continuous improvement.

The process is organized into seven core phases, each with dedicated documentation, templates, and checklists to ensure consistency and quality across all projects.

## Brief Summary of Processes

- **Initiation**: Capture the problem statement, identify stakeholders, and create a one-pager to make a go/no-go decision before investing in detailed planning.
- **Planning**: Break work into shippable increments with a prioritized backlog, estimates, release plan, and clear definition of done.
- **Execution & Tracking**: Maintain team rhythm through daily standups and weekly syncs, manage work on a project board, follow PR and CI practices, and report progress and metrics.
- **Release & Deployment**: Execute releases safely with pre-release requirements, deployment checklists, smoke tests, and documented rollback playbooks.
- **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement**: Run structured retrospectives after sprints or releases to capture learnings and convert them into actionable improvements.
- **Risk Management & Communication**: Maintain a risk register, proactively communicate status to stakeholders, and escalate issues through defined paths.
- **Roles & Personas**: Define clear responsibilities for Project Managers, Product Managers, Developers, QA/Testing, and Stakeholders.

## Core Principles

- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability in all decisions.
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments to reduce risk and gather feedback early.
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has a named Project Manager and Product Lead.
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence.
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback, learning, and blameless retrospectives.

## Key Artifacts

All OctoAcme projects should maintain:
- **Project Charter / One-pager** — problem statement, goals, success metrics, stakeholders, and timeline
- **Roadmap and Release Plan** — milestones, dependencies, and delivery dates
- **Sprint/Iteration Backlog** — prioritized list of work with acceptance criteria and estimates
- **Risk Register** — tracked risks with impact, likelihood, mitigation, and status
- **Project Board** — visual workflow tracking progress through Backlog → Ready → In Progress → In Review → QA → Done
- **Retrospective Notes** — learnings and action items from each sprint or release

## Communication Cadence

- **Daily**: 15-minute standups with delivery team
- **Weekly**: PM + Product Lead sync, delivery team standup, risk register review
- **Bi-weekly**: Stakeholder updates (or as agreed with sponsors)
- **Sprint/Release**: Demos, reviews, retrospectives, and lessons-learned sessions
- **As-needed**: Escalations, incident communication, and decision approvals
